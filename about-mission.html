Our Mission & Foundation

Anchored in community stewardship, worker well-being, and sustainable environmental impact through the Koinonia Grace Foundation.

Rooted in Purpose: Beyond the Evergreen

While Christmas Tree Joe redefines the modern holiday experience through effortless doorstep delivery, our core heartbeat lives on ChristmasTreeJoe.info. We believe that true luxury and convenience should never come at the expense of our agricultural workers or our environment.

Operated in alignment with the Koinonia Grace Foundation and connected through Black and Brown Laura USA, our initiative bridges commercial excellence with deep-rooted community stewardship, ensuring that every tree harvested contributes directly to the uplifting of the people and ecosystems behind the industry.

Our Three Core Pillars

🌱 The Recycle Park Initiative

We champion full-lifecycle environmental responsibility. Through our post-holiday collection and community Recycle Park programs, we ensure that evergreens are sustainably repurposed—keeping organic waste out of municipal landfills and enriching local ecosystems.

🤝 Worker & Farmer Well-Being

The evergreen industry relies heavily on the hard work of dedicated growers, farmhands, and field laborers. We advocate for and invest in the health, safety, fair treatment, and economic security of the people who cultivate our Appalachian Fraser Firs.

💝 Community Grants & Support

Powered by the Koinonia Grace Foundation, we direct charitable contributions and grants toward targeted community programs, educational initiatives, and localized support networks for working families.
