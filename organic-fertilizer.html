Regenerative Soil Science • Nitrogen-Balanced Composting

Legacy Mulch & Soil Nutrients

Repurposing evergreen wood fiber through biological composting, microbial inoculation, and slow-release bio-nutrient synthesis returned to local farms.

The Science of Composting Softwoods

Balancing the Carbon-Nitrogen Ratio

Process 01

Precision Chipping

Trunks and dense limbs are chipped into uniform 1/2-inch flakes, creating an optimal surface area for bacterial colonization and moisture absorption.

Process 02

Mycorrhizal Inoculation

Wood chips are inoculated with beneficial fungal spores and organic nitrogen sources to neutralize resin acidity and prevent nitrogen robbery in farm topsoil.

Process 03

Aged Humus Maturation

Aged through controlled thermophilic windrows over 180 days, producing rich, weed-free organic humus that retains 40% more soil moisture.

Agricultural Output

Soil Remediation Outputs

Legacy Organic Wood Mulch

Ground Cover & Moisture Shield

Non-dyed, chemical-free tree bark and trunk mulch designed for urban landscaping, botanical gardens, and community reforestation zones. Suppresses weeds and moderates root temperatures year-round.

  • Zero artificial petroleum colorants
  • High-retention porous fiber structure
  • Donated freely to municipal parks and community gardens

Bio-Active Conifer Compost Blend

Living Soil Micro-Nutrient Matrix

Enriched with decomposed conifer needles, biochar, and composted wood fiber. Returned directly to certified tree farms to fertilize new generations of seedlings at no cost to independent growers.

  • Restores organic carbon levels in agricultural soil
  • Reduces the need for synthetic NPK fertilizers
  • 100% Closed-loop agricultural return

Closing the Loop on Conifer Soil

Every ton of processed organic fertilizer directly finances free compost shipments back to small-acreage family tree farms, ensuring fertile ground for the next decade of holiday conifers.
