🚜 Comprehensive Sector Ledger

Where Every Dollar Operates

An open accounting of how foundation funds are distributed across the agricultural ecosystem.

🦺
Sector 01 • Occupational Health

Harvester Gear & Ergonomics

Harvesting softwoods in sub-freezing temperatures requires specialized safety equipment. Grants supply Kevlar chainsaw chaps, cold-weather grip gloves, ergonomic shearing knives, and safety harness rigging.

Average Package: $180 / Worker
🧪
Sector 02 • Soil Science

Microbiome & Organic Nutrients

To eliminate toxic synthetic chemicals, grants provide farmers with mycorrhizal fungal inoculants, organic slow-release composted mulch, and quarterly soil lab assays measuring organic matter and root-depth health.

Annual Assay & Inputs: $450 / Acre
🌲
Sector 03 • Silviculture Banks

Sapling Stock & Seedbeds

Enforcing the mandatory 3:1 reforestation covenant requires steady sapling supplies. Grants purchase certified root-stock seedlings (Fraser, Balsam, Canaan, and Noble Firs) distributed free to small growers.

Replanting Subsidy: 100% Covered
⚙️
Sector 04 • Farm Mechanization

Tractor Upkeep & Netting Balers

Small farmers rely on vintage tractors, mechanical tree balers, and stump grinders. Emergency micro-grants fund mechanical repairs and biodegradable cotton twine baling supplies during the critical 30-day harvest.

Emergency Repair Fund: Up to $1,200 / Farm
♻️
Sector 05 • Circular Infrastructure

Mobile Distillers & Heavy Chippers

Financing municipal collection drop sites, commercial tree shredders, and mobile steam hydro-distillation trailers that extract pine terpenes for non-toxic Clean Blue Green™ botanical cleaning formulas.

Zero-Landfill Processing: 100% Closed-Loop
🤝
Sector 06 • Labor Equity

Fair Wage & Health Stipends

Independent farms operating in economically depressed rural pockets receive direct wage parity subsidies to ensure every full-time and seasonal worker earns above the regional living wage benchmark.
