The Complete Lifecycle

Effortless Holiday Fulfillment

Step 01

Curated Slot Booking

Subscribers choose their exact arrival window starting in mid-November, receiving live driver dispatch updates.

Step 02

Climate-Controlled Transit

Transported straight from regenerative tree farms in protective netting to prevent moisture depletion and branch stress.

Step 03

In-Room White Glove Setup

Technicians unnet the tree, secure it into a heavy-duty stand, perform a fresh trunk slice, and provide initial hydration.

Step 04

Clean Return & Recycling

Scheduled post-holiday takedown and removal. 100% of organic mass is redirected to The Recycle Park for zero landfill waste.
