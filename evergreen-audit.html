Evergreen Seal™ Certification

The premier ecological audit standard for conifer growers, municipal drop sites, and enterprise distributors—guaranteeing living wages, biological soil health, and 100% circular post-holiday recovery.

Certification Framework

The 4 Pillars of the Evergreen Seal™

Standard 01

3:1 Reforestation

Mandatory replanting of at least three genetically verified evergreen saplings for every single conifer harvested, maintaining net canopy expansion.

Standard 02

Labor Equity & Safety

Verified living wages, occupational protective gear compliance, paid safety education, and sanitary working conditions for seasonal agricultural crews.

Standard 03

Biological Soil Care

Strict limits on synthetic chemical runoff, enforcement of integrated pest management (IPM), and regular mycorrhizal microbial soil testing.

Standard 04

Zero-Landfill Chain

Audited chain-of-custody verifying that post-season retail trees are retrieved and directed to circular bio-refining at The Recycle Park.
