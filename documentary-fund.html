The Narrative Structure

What This Film Captures

Part I

The Decade of Silence

Chronicling the year-round challenges growers face through eight harsh winters, summer droughts, and biological pests before a tree reaches holiday maturity.

Field Production (Ongoing)
Part II

The 30-Day Harvest Rush

Behind-the-scenes access to the high-stakes logistical surge that distributes millions of conifers to homes and city squares in a compressed four-week window.

Principal Photography
Part III

The Circular Resurrection

Investigating what happens on January 2nd—following the transformation of discarded trees into bio-nutrients, clean chemistry, and renewed forest soil.

Post-Holiday Research
