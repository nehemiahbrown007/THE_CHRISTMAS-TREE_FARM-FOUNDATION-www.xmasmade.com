The Stewards of the Soil

Behind every holiday evergreen is a decade of agricultural devotion, climate resilience, and generational knowledge.

The Grower's Journey

A Decade in the Making

Years 1–3

Seedling & Root Establishment

Nurtured in protective nurseries before precision field transplantation. Farmers manage micro-irrigation and balance soil mycorrhizae against harsh spring frosts.

Years 4–7

Hand-Shearing & Shaping

Every branch is hand-sheared annually to develop optimal density, straight central leaders, and traditional symmetrical conifer architecture.

Years 8–10

Harvest & Reforestation

Sustainable selective harvesting followed immediately by soil regeneration and replanting at a minimum 3:1 seedling replacement ratio.

Advocacy in Action

How The Foundation Supports Growers

Occupational Health & Safety

Subsidies for protective gear, specialized field equipment, and year-round safety certifications for seasonal agricultural crews.

Soil Health & Biology Grants

Non-repayable micro-grants for zero-chemical soil remediation, organic nutrient management, and cover crop integration.

10-Year Income Stabilization

Pairing growers with long-term consumer subscription commitments to secure reliable upfront crop financing.

Documentary Storytelling

Preserving the cultural heritage of independent family farms through the "10 Years for 1 Month" historical documentary.

Preserve the Legacy of Our Growers

Your direct contribution to the Farmer Support Fund goes straight to field safety, soil health testing, and documentary archiving.
