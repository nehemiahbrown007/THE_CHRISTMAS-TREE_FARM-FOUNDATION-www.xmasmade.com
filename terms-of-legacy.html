Terms of Legacy & Covenant

The governing legal framework, 10-year subscription covenant protections, and ecological stewardship responsibilities of our network.

1. Philanthropic Purpose & Network Alignment

The Christmas Tree Farm Foundation operates to safeguard generational conifer agriculture, provide direct farmer safety subsidies, and eliminate holiday timber waste. By interacting with the Foundation, contributing donations, or enrolling in conifer subscription suites via accredited retail partners such as Christmas Tree Joe, you agree to these operating standards.

2. The 10-Year Subscription Covenant

Subscribers to the 10-Year Plan lock in conifer fulfillment over a ten-consecutive-year duration. The Foundation acts as the verification authority, ensuring:

  • Annual fulfillment of healthy, premium-grade Fraser, Noble, or Balsam fir trees sourced exclusively from audited farms.
  • Protection against market price increases, guaranteed through dedicated parcel escrow and grower upfront crop funding.
  • Mandatory white-glove collection and certified routing to The Recycle Park for zero-landfill post-holiday processing.

3. Mandatory Reforestation & Agricultural Standards

All affiliated growers agree to the mandatory 3:1 replacement covenant. For every tree harvested for seasonal retail distribution, three seedlings must be replanted in accredited soil within the designated planting calendar window. Failure to maintain verified sapling planting density triggers automatic decertification under the Evergreen Seal™ framework.

4. Intellectual Property & Digital Assets

All engineering blueprints, chemical distillation protocols, soil testing methodologies, and documentary media (including "10 Years for 1 Month" production footage) accessed via the Digital Vault remain protected under proprietary licensing. Commercial reproduction of proprietary formulas without Foundation certification is strictly prohibited.

5. Limitation of Liability & Environmental Force Majeure

While the Foundation enforces strict silviculture and logistics redundancy across regional farm co-ops, agricultural yields are subject to severe climate anomalies, unseasonable frosts, and natural events. In circumstances of severe local shortages, equivalent species from certified partner regions within our global network will be allocated to fulfill subscriber covenants.

6. Governing Jurisdiction

These Terms of Legacy and any dispute arising from Foundation programs, grants, or platform usage shall be governed in accordance with applicable state and federal laws governing non-profit operations and agricultural stewardship agreements.
