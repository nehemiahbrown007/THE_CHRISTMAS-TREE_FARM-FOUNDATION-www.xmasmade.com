Conifer Chemistry & Eco-Cleaners

Extracting high-potency terpenes, pinene solvents, and essential oils from reclaimed evergreen needles to formulate non-toxic botanical cleaning solutions.

Closed-Loop Refining

From Needle to Natural Solvent

Phase 01

Steam Hydro-Distillation

Needles separated at The Recycle Park undergo low-temperature steam hydro-distillation, capturing volatile conifer terpenes without thermal degradation.

Phase 02

Alpha-Pinene Isolation

Natural alpha-pinene and limonene fractions are isolated—yielding food-safe grease-cutting solvents and natural antimicrobial agents.

Phase 03

Clean Blue Green™ Blending

Combined with organic surfactants and plant hydrosols to formulate high-performance surface cleaners, degreasers, and forest-scented home concentrates.

100% Plant-Derived Active Solvent
